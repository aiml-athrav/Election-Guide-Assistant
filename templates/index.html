Election Process Timeline

A simple step-by-step guide to how general elections work.

1. Official Announcement

The Election Commission officially declares the dates for nominations, polling, and results.

2. Voter Registration

Citizens must ensure their names are on the voter rolls. New voters can apply for their Voter ID cards during this period.

3. Polling Day

Registered voters go to their designated polling booths, verify their identity, and cast their secret ballot.

4. Counting & Results

Votes from all machines/ballots are tallied centrally, and the final winners are officially announced.
